Common Questions

"What exactly is a Homes That Heal Starter Kit?"

A completely custom solution that starts with your wellness kitchen. Every Starter Kit is uniquely designed for your family, beginning with the heart of your home—your kitchen. We design harvest processing, fermentation spaces, gathering areas, and nourishing meal preparation, then expand through all dimensions: wellness architecture, systems integration, and regenerative infrastructure. This is an intimate process guided by our team of experts.

"Is this ideal for homesteading projects?"

Yes—Homes That Heal Starter Kits are actually ideal for homesteading. If you're already homesteading, we can help you design and implement ADUs (Accessory Dwelling Units) with the highest ecological standards and technological efficiency. Whether it's a guest house, family compound addition, or retreat space on your homestead, we bring German Kitchen Center's wellness kitchen expertise and regenerative systems integration to your land.

"Can you help me design a new ecovillage?"

Yes. We believe ecovillages and the 'agrihood' movement are among the most critical investments to impact the betterment of life on Earth. We are developing a new standard for building the future of wellness with Homes That Heal—where every dwelling, from wellness kitchens to integrated home systems, creates regenerative communities that demonstrate another way of living is possible.

"Why start with the kitchen?"

The kitchen is where life happens. It's where food becomes nourishment, where family gathers, where wellness begins. German Kitchen Center has spent over 20 years perfecting this craft. Your wellness kitchen becomes the foundation—connecting to gardens, processing harvests, supporting fermentation, enabling meal preparation that heals. Everything else expands from this heart.

"How customized is each project?"

Completely customized—aligned to your family and community. Your dedicated designer coordinates our expert team to create a solution specific to you.
