| "Can I hire contractors separately?" | Fragmented approaches create expensive mistakes | Pain amplification + integration value |

**Brand Script Alignment:** Objection preemption with value reframing (Refusal of the Call stage)

## 📝 BRAND VOICE CHECKLIST

When creating future content, ensure:

- ✅ Grounded tone (like soil—stable, nourishing, real)
- ✅ Wise perspective (pattern recognition, systems thinking)
- ✅ Inviting stance (companion, not guru)
- ✅ Precise claims (sensory details, tangible outcomes)
- ✅ Hopeful realism (challenges acknowledged, solutions emphasized)

### Avoid:
- ❌ Academic jargon or pretentious eco-speak
- ❌ Judgment of conventional homeowners
- ❌ Overpromising magical thinking
- ❌ Fear-based marketing
- ❌ Corporate greenwashing language
